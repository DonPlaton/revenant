CAST, SHEET ONE

The revenant

The figure on the app icon, redrawn so the parts that carry its life can move on separate clocks. It flees left; everything about the drawing says so, including where it is looking.

eyespupils sit back and right,
so it watches what follows
moutha held breath, gasping
on a slower clock
hemfive points hung like cloth,
swinging 80ms behind each other
forma lit leading edge and
a shaded trailing one
speed linesmotion, not identity:
they stay out of the icon

The body and all five hem peaks are shared character for character with assets/icon.svg, so the mascot and the installed icon cannot drift apart. A test asserts it.
